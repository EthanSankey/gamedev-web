Laridae Deliveries

Laridae Deliveries was a collaborative development project that provided valuable experience working within a multidisciplinary team environment. As the Design Leader, I was responsible for overseeing the game's design direction, coordinating design decisions, and ensuring that the team's vision remained consistent throughout development.

This role allowed me to develop both leadership and communication skills whilst contributing to the overall creative process. In addition to guiding the project's gameplay and design decisions, I worked closely with other team members to address challenges, balance priorities, and maintain a cohesive experience. Through this project, I gained a deeper understanding of collaborative game development and the importance of effective teamwork when delivering a successful final product.
